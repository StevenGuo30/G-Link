G-Link city artwork extraction
Tool: built-in image_gen (transparent_background=true).
Inputs: four city illustrations supplied by the repository owner.
The original downloads are preserved outside this repository.

Chicago
Use case: background-extraction. Input image is the EDIT TARGET, the supplied Chicago architectural illustration for G-Link. Extract the existing entire skyline as a single clean transparent-background PNG asset. Preserve the exact buildings, landmark shapes, relative placement, facade details, ice-blue colors and existing warm highlights. Remove ALL sky, clouds, water, reflections, distant mountains, white border lines and the stray duplicate image strips along the top/left edges. Keep only buildings, their architectural base and the very thin immediate foreground tree strip, if present. Everything above and between building silhouettes must have genuine alpha transparency, not a colored or checkerboard background. Keep all antenna tips and spires intact, no building cropped off. Keep the full wide panorama and align building bases near the bottom; only a very small transparent padding below the buildings. Make the building group occupy most of the canvas height while retaining the original wide aspect ratio. Do NOT redesign, relight, add architecture, text, labels, borders, fog or sky. This is an asset extraction from the supplied image, not a new city illustration.
